Engines in Railroader have four major controls:

The _independent_ and _train_ brakes behave very differently and are used in different situations.

The independent brake controls the brakes on the engine and tender only.

## Train Brake

The _train brake_ is used for slowing or stopping a _train_. It's controlled by changing the air pressure in the brake line,

## The Throttle and The Reverser

The _throttle_ controls how much steam is sent to the pistons, which makes the engine go faster.
When it is closed, 0%, no steam is sent to the pistons. At 100%, maximum steam is sent.

The _reverser_ controls how the pistons apply force the drivers by changing how the _valve gear_
operates. This makes it possible to control direction as well as more efficiently use the steam
at higher speeds.
===
## Регулятор и реверс

_Регулятор_ определяет, сколько пара поступает в цилиндры: чем больше пара, тем быстрее идёт локомотив.
Когда он закрыт (0%), пар в цилиндры не поступает. При 100% подаётся максимум пара.

_Реверс_ определяет, как поршни передают усилие на движущие колёса, меняя работу
_парораспределительного механизма_. С его помощью можно менять направление движения и экономнее расходовать пар
на высоких скоростях.

speeds. As your speed rises you'll need to move the reverser closer to the center to get full power.
===
Когда реверс полностью вперёд (100%), пар толкает локомотив вперёд.

## More about the HUD

Now that the locomotive is selected, the HUD controls appear in the lower left.

The HUD also provides the standard engine controls, and can be used from miles away
whether you're in first person or the overhead view.

In the HUD, forward in the engine is right (apply brakes, close throttle),
and back is left (release brakes, open throttle).

The HUD also shows details about your engine, including its number, weight, speed and
the state of its brakes (the dots below the engine number). In the brake display each
dot represents a car in your train. The color shows whether that car's brakes are released
(white), applied (red), or handbrake applied (purple). Derailed cars are dark gray.

The numbers on the brake sliders indicate the brake line pressure and brake cylinder pressure.
These are also shown on gauges inside the cab.

## Speed Limits

Railroader doesn't enforce strict speed limits, but going above the speed limit on curves can damage your equipment,
as well as potentially cause the train to derail at excessive speeds,
so it's a good idea to start out slow as you're learning the railroad.
The tighter the curve or switch, the lower the safe speed!

- Most of the mainline is good for 35mph, except where noted by yellow speed signs. These signs do not give you much warning, however, so check the map or look ahead as you're learning the railroad.
- Mainline siding switches are good for 25mph or better.
- Yard, industrial, and branch switches should be treated as 15mph.

The mainline between Whittier and Ela is good for 35, though there is one sharp curve that needs to be taken at 30.
===
## Ограничения скорости

Railroader не следит строго за ограничениями скорости, но превышение в кривых может повредить подвижной состав,
а на слишком высокой скорости поезд может и сойти с рельсов,
поэтому, пока вы изучаете дорогу, лучше ездить медленно.
Чем круче кривая или стрелка, тем ниже безопасная скорость!

- Почти на всём главном пути можно ехать 35 миль/ч, кроме мест, отмеченных жёлтыми знаками ограничения скорости. Но эти знаки предупреждают поздно, так что, пока изучаете дорогу, сверяйтесь с картой или смотрите вперёд.
- Стрелки разъездов на главном пути можно проходить на 25 миль/ч и быстрее.
- Станционные, подъездные и стрелки на ветках считайте рассчитанными на 15 миль/ч.

Главный путь между Whittier и Ela рассчитан на 35, но там есть одна крутая кривая, которую нужно проходить на 30.

## The Run Around

The run around is one of the most common moves in railroading. The goal is simple: move your locomotive
(and perhaps some cars) to the other end of the train. You can do a run around anywhere there's
a double-ended siding.

Once the passengers to Whittier have loaded,
before we return to Whittier, pull your passenger car west, past the switch. Disconnect from it and
run your engine down to the west end of the siding. Use the switches to run back around to the east end
of the passenger car, and couple back on to it. Don't forget to hook up the air!

The engine will run tender-first back to Whittier.
Before it does, though, let's look at our freight options in the next chapter.

## Pass some time

When you're finished with the day's work in Railroader, the logical next step is to get
some sleep. Sleeping allows time to pass until the interchange is served, at which point
all of the outgoing cars that had been delivered to the interchange are taken away,
and all of the incoming cars are delivered, ready for you to take them to the customer.

## Freight Car Life Cycle

Now that this car is spotted, the customer will load or unload it, which could take
a few hours or a few days, depending on the customer and tier.

When the customer is done with the car a new waybill will be assigned to it to return it to the interchange.
You'll want to check your customers daily for cars with new waybills.
Remember that unfulfilled waybills are also listed in the station agent window.

This is the core of freight operations - picking up cars from the interchange and
delivering them to their proper destinations. As you continue,
you'll get more efficient at handling multiple cars and planning your moves.

Once the day's work is done, sleep until the next interchange service,
or wait until later in the day and check your customers -- perhaps they have finished
unloading that car and it's ready to return to the interchange?
You could also consider increasing some of your contract tiers.
===

доставлять их по назначению. Со временем вы научитесь
ловчее управляться с несколькими вагонами и планировать маневры.

## Captive Service

Some industries use captive service cars to move loads. If you want to serve the saw mill,
you will use your log skeleton cars in _captive service_ to load logs at one of the Connelly Creek tracks,
and unload them at Whittier Saw Mill.

Captive service cars are owned by your railroad and work the same as the hopper and box car:
set the Unload and Load destinations to determine where they go to be unloaded and loaded.
Note that you must have a contract with a customer before you can select its tracks as a destination.
You can purchase additional cars to increase your capacity.

Unlike freight that moves through the interchange, you set the pace at which captive service cars move.
See the Location tab information for the industry for guidance on how many car loads it expects.

Captive service deliveries are paid at midnight.
===
